Questions? Answered.

Do I need to know 3D modeling or CAD?

Nope — that's the whole point. Answer two questions, pick a shape, and the mold builds itself. Every setting has a plain-English explanation, and the defaults are already right for your craft.

What do I need to use my mold?

A 3D printer (or a friend/library/print shop with one) and your casting material — wax, soap base, resin, chocolate, or plaster. Print the mold in PLA or PETG, then pour. For flexible food-safe molds, print our two-part shell and pour silicone into it.

Can I sell things I make with my molds?

With the Pro, Lifetime, or Day Pass plans, yes — and unlike other tools, your commercial license for everything you made while subscribed stays valid even if you cancel. We think that's just fair.

Are my designs uploaded to your servers?

No. All the 3D work happens right in your browser on your device. Your shapes and files are never sent anywhere — which also makes it fast.

Does the free plan really have no watermark?

Really. 10 clean downloads a day, forever. We'd rather win you over with the product than hold your files hostage.

Can I use my own 3D files?

Yes — upload any STL file and we'll build a mold around it. Big files welcome; the "Will it pop out?" check warns you about tricky shapes before you print.
